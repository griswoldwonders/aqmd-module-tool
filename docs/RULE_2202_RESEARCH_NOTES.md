# Rule 2202 Research Notes

**Sources accessed:** August 27, 2026.

## Official South Coast AQMD overview

Source: https://www.aqmd.gov/home/programs/business/business-detail?title=rule-2202-on-road-motor-vehicle-mitigation-options

The official overview states that Rule 2202 provides employers with a menu of options to reduce mobile-source emissions from employee commutes. It states that employers in the South Coast Air Basin with 250 or more employees at a worksite must notify South Coast AQMD of subjectivity, including worksite address, highest-ranking official contact information, site contact information, and employee count. Once AQMD receives the notice, the worksite has 90 days to implement a compliance option and provide a registration demonstrating compliance; annual registration follows on that date thereafter. The page links to Rule 2202 forms/guidelines, FIND status, ETC training, and a summary activity report.

## Official forms and guidelines page

Source: https://www.aqmd.gov/home/programs/business/r2202-forms-guidelines

The official page lists the Rule 2202 rule, Employee Commute Reduction Program Guidelines, Implementation Guidelines, Rule 2202(f)(6) other-emission-reduction-strategy procedures, emission-factor methodology documents, 2026 emission-factor tables valid for registrations submitted after December 31, 2025, AVR survey support guidance, random-sample AVR guidance, compliance forms, and survey forms. The implementation-guidelines description says it outlines the framework, calculation methods, and criteria used in determining emission-reduction credits and vehicle-trip emission credits for Rule 2202 compliance.

## Product implications

Relay Rider should be designed as an evidence and workflow system, not as an autonomous compliance authority. It should preserve source documents and methodology versions, record worksite applicability and registration dates, support annual reporting periods, distinguish verified employer/employee inputs from modeled values, and generate draft submissions for responsible review. The engine should not claim that an employer is compliant merely because the app calculates a result.

The current repository already contains Rule 2202 workspace and evidence concepts, but the compliance engine needs versioned methodology tables, source provenance, calculation inputs, review/approval states, audit events, report-generation artifacts, and a clear “draft / verified / submitted / accepted” lifecycle.
